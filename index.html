In plain language

Step-free

No stairs or kerbs to climb. Ramps and lifts are fine.

Lift status

Whether a lift works. Here it is “unknown” until someone checks.

Last checked

The date someone confirmed the route in person. Demo data says “sample”.

Trust

This depends on route audits and updated campus information. Every report and route check should show a date, evidence and a status: Unverified, Reviewed or Verified on site.

About the project

P12 – The Ramp That Ends at a Step

How might we make the journey from a hostel to a classroom reliably barrier-free for people with mobility constraints?

Early research themes

Our initial survey had 22 responses, mostly students. It is exploratory — early signals, not campus-wide statistics.

Lift access

Availability and distance came up often.

Continuous step-free routes

Steep or missing ramps and uneven surfaces.

Route information

Current details are hard to find.

Detours

Closures add time and effort.

Reporting

Students want a simple way to flag problems.

Wanted

Closure notices, route photos, “last checked” dates.

What we still need to learn

  • Audit real routes on site
  • Interview students with varied mobility needs
  • Speak with facilities staff
  • Verify lift and closure information
